# Archaeologic

Archaeologic contains a series of archive publications and documents pertinent to the history of microcomputing in Britain. All of the documents collected here are out of print. A number of them were published by organisations which are now defunct. As such the status of their ownership, from a copyright perspective, is uncertain. Other documents are currently within copyright, but I have chosen to make copies available because of their, in my view, overarching value to the historian.

If you are the owner of one or more of the documents contained here, please contact me. I am happy to discuss their ongoing availability.

Tony Smith
